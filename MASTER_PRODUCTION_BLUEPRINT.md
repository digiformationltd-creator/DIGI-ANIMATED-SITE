### 1. CINEMA ENGINE ARCHITECTURE (`RealisticCinemaEngine`)
Adopted and elevated from `TEA SITE/app/components/journey/RealisticCinemaEngine.tsx`:
1. **Spring-Damped Scrubbing Loop**:
   ```typescript
   currentTimes[index] += (target - currentTimes[index]) * 0.18;
   video.currentTime = clamp(currentTimes[index], 0, 0.996) * video.duration;
   ```
2. **Sigmoid Cross-Dissolve Transitions**:
   Transitions between scenes execute via smoothstep sigmoid curves (`fade * fade * (3 - 2 * fade)`), ensuring zero jarring cuts and total camera continuity.
3. **Atmospheric Audio Generator**:
   Procedural binaural pink noise filtered with lowpass biquad filters to simulate high-end office acoustics, air conditioning hum, server room whirr, and crisp document handling.
